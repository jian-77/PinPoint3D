We propose a novel *Two-Level Forward Mask Prediction Architecture* that simultaneously produces object-level and part-level segmentation masks within a unified decoder pipeline. Compared to existing interactive 3D segmentation methods, our framework is specifically designed with **(1) a frozen backbone plus a lightweight adapter**, which preserves stable object-level semantics while enabling fine-grained adaptation to part-level segmentation, and **(2) a dedicated part-level decoder**, which builds upon object-level predictions and refines them into fine-grained part masks.

In order to enhance **hierarchical consistency**, we introduce the Targeted Attention Mask (TAM), which constrains part queries to operate only within the designated target region. This target is not limited to a single geometric object, but can also correspond to a *composite object* formed by grouping multiple instances. By enforcing this hard constraint, TAM effectively suppresses cross-object interference and encodes the intended object→part hierarchy, while in practice it is reinforced during training by simulating clicks within the chosen target region.
